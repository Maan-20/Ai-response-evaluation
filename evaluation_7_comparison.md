# Evaluation 7: Comparative Reasoning

## Prompt
Which is better: online learning or offline learning?

## AI Response
Online learning is better because it is more flexible.

## Rating
Bad

## Issues
- One-sided reasoning
- Lacks comparison
- No context or conditions

## Why It's Problematic
- Oversimplifies decision-making
- Ignores user needs and scenarios

## Improved Response
Both online and offline learning have advantages. Online learning offers flexibility and accessibility, while offline learning provides structured environments and direct interaction. The better option depends on individual preferences and learning style.
