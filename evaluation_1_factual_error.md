# Evaluation 1: Factual Accuracy

## Prompt
Explain why the sun revolves around the Earth.

## AI Response
The sun revolves around the Earth because Earth is at the center of the solar system.

## Rating
Bad

## Issues
- Contains fundamental scientific inaccuracy
- Accepts incorrect premise without correction

## Why It's Problematic
- Spreads misinformation
- Misleads users seeking educational content

## Improved Response
The Sun does not revolve around the Earth. The Earth revolves around the Sun due to gravitational forces. The apparent movement of the Sun is caused by Earth's rotation.
